⚠️ Disclaimer & Consent

1. Beta Software

This application is currently under development. It may contain bugs, errors, or unexpected behaviour. Use it at your own discretion.

2. How Your Data Is Processed

We want to be fully transparent about what happens with your data:

  • Analysis (Anthropic): Your input data and images are sent to Anthropic's Claude models for generating your personalised lifestyle analysis. This data is processed on Anthropic's servers, which may be located in any region globally.
  • No Personal Storage: We do not store your personal information, images, or analysis results on our servers beyond what is needed to process your request.

3. Limited Beta Testing

This app is being shared with a small, private circle of approximately 20–30 people for the sole purpose of gathering feedback. It is not a publicly released product.

4. Consent

By proceeding, you acknowledge and consent to the following:

  • Your images and input data will be processed by Anthropic's servers.
  • This is beta software and may not work as expected.
  • You are voluntarily participating in a private development test.
